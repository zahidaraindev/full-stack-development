About us

A academy built by practitioners, for beginners

Spectrum Academy opened in 2019 with two rented classrooms and one course. Today we run six full-time programmes and a growing evening batch schedule, but the approach hasn't changed: learn by doing, from someone who does it for a living.

Mission

Make skilled instruction affordable and local

To give students a practical, job-ready skill in a subject they choose — taught close to home, at a fee families can plan for.

Vision

A campus for every kind of learner

To be the first academy families think of for technology, design, language, and science education in the region.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to

🎓 Practical first

Every course is built around a project or portfolio piece, not just theory.

🤝 Small and personal

Batches stay small enough that instructors know every student by name.

📈 Honest progress

Clear, regular feedback — students always know where they stand.

🌍 Open to all

Flexible fee plans and evening batches so more students can attend.
